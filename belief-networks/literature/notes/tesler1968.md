# Notes: A Directed Graph Representation for Computer Simulation of Belief Systems

Tesler, Lawrence; Enea, Horace; Colby, Kenneth Mark (1968). Mathematical Biosciences. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0025556468900047

2 marks in 2 passages, in page order. Page numbers are those of the PDF. Marked words are in bold; the text around them is the sentence before and the sentence after.

**p. 3.** Kinds of concepts: 1. Sets; 2. Individuals; 3. Propositions. **A belief in this model is an attitude toward a proposition about concepts.** Examples of propositions are men like women, John obeys his father, fear of men leads to avoidance of men.

**p. 5.** In addition, solution of some nonlinguistic problems, such as the determination of relevance, may be expedited by regarding them as context problems. **It has already been indicated that beliefs are attitudes toward propositions and that propositions are special kinds of concepts.** This reduces the problem mainly to one of representing concepts, their values, and the relations between them.
