**p. 3.** That said, we would argue that the conceptual framework underlying our approach provides a more robust basis for the realisation of news analytics solutions than the one adopted by Chen et al. **In particular, in their approach, there is no explicit characterisation of a topic and the process of identifying relevant perspectives is driven by the selection of a particular claim**. **Here, it could be argued that it is unclear what is the epistemological basis for distinguishing between a claim and a perspective**. More importantly, **while a claim implies a topic, the lack of an explicit characterisation of the current topic makes it diﬃcult to map the space of relevant viewpoints. Indeed, in contrast with our approach, there is no attempt in [7] at providing such a characterisation of the space of viewpoints**. On the contrary, topics, claims and viewpoints play clearly distinctive roles in our framework, which is grounded on the formal analysis and ontology presented in [8].
