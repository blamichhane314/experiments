# Notes: Differences Between Belief and Knowledge Systems

Abelson, Robert P. (1979). Cognitive Science. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1207/s15516709cog0304_4

3 marks in 3 passages, in page order. Page numbers are those of the PDF. Marked words are in bold; the text around them is the sentence before and the sentence after.

**p. 1.** **Seven features which in practice seem to differentiate belief systems from knowledge systems are discussed.** These are: nonconsensuality, “existence beliefs,” alternative worlds, evaluative components, episodic material, unboundedness, and variable credences.

**p. 2.** Any system embodying most of them, however, will have the essential character of a “belief system”: 1. **The elements (concepts, propositions, rules, etc.)** of a belief system are not consensual. That is, the elements of one system might be quite different from those of a second in the same content domain.

**p. 3.** Carbonell’s (1978) POLITICS program does both of these things to some extent. 2. **Belief systems are in part concerned with the existence or nonexistence of certain conceptual entities.** God, ESP, witches, and assassination conspiracies are examples of such entities.
